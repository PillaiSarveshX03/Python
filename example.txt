Missile Categories
==================
- Ballistic Missiles: Follow a high, arcing trajectory after launch.
- Cruise Missiles: Fly within the atmosphere using continuous propulsion.
- Surface-to-Air Missiles (SAMs): Defend against aircraft and incoming missiles.
- Anti-Ship Missiles: Designed to engage naval vessels.
- Anti-Tank Guided Missiles (ATGMs): Used against armored vehicles.

Note:
The ranges listed are approximate and may vary by variant or official updates.
